Core AI Processing Methodology

Step 01

Webcam & File Stream

Captures live WebRTC camera frames or uploaded images of any aspect ratio (1920x1080, 500x800, 1:1, etc.).

Step 02

RetinaFace Detection

Locates face bounding boxes and predicts 5 key facial landmarks (left eye, right eye, nose, left mouth, right mouth).

Step 03

Affine Face Alignment

Applies 2D similarity transform mapping 5 facial landmarks to a standard target template, eliminating head tilt and roll.

Step 04

ArcFace Embedding

Deep neural network transforms aligned 112x112 face chip into a unit-normalized 512-dimensional numerical vector.

Step 05

Cosine Similarity Match

Computes dot product between query embedding and stored database vectors with maximum sample score aggregation.

Step 06

Real-Time Render

Draws bounding box, landmarks, name tag, confidence meter, and MATCH/UNKNOWN status directly on live stream.
